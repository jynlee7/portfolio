Clinical Intervention Simulator

A Monte Carlo framework that models 300 patient deterioration scenarios, feeding a Random Forest that optimizes interventions in real time.

Screenshot · 21:9

The simulation is vectorized with NumPy so a full sweep runs in seconds rather than minutes, which is what made it usable as an interactive tool instead of a batch job. It ships as a Streamlit app over a Pandas ETL pipeline that reconciles multimodal health data into one frame.

Built at the 7th Annual Datathon at Berkeley, where it took first place against more than 50 teams and over 200 participants.
